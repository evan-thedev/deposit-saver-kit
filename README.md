# Deposit Saver Kit

A $4.99 printable move-in / move-out inspection kit for renters. One PDF (US Letter, ~12 pages) plus an optional Google Sheet for photo logging and a deposit-deadline countdown.

> Walk out with dated proof, a clean unit checklist, and the paperwork trail that makes "we're keeping your deposit" harder to pull.

**Status:** planning. No PDF, Sheet, listing, or code exists yet. The plan is awaiting approval before the PDF is built.

## What's in the kit (when built)

- Side-by-side move-in / move-out room sheets with a Photo ID# column
- Keys, fobs & remotes log; meter reading log
- Move-out cleaning checklist ordered by what landlords actually charge for
- Wear vs. damage cheat sheet (general guidance, not legal advice)
- Photo protocol + blank photo log
- Texas-aware 30-day timeline, works anywhere
- Three email templates, including the written forwarding-address email
- Google Sheet: `Photo_Log`, `Deadline` (target return date formula), `Instructions`

## Principles

- Pure digital download. Zero ops after listing.
- No web app, no accounts, no Stripe code beyond a Payment Link, no state-law database, no landlord tools.
- Tenant-first. Practical forms, not legal advice.
- Personal use, one household.
