## FAQ (paste into Etsy FAQ section and use for support replies)

**Is this a physical product?**
No. Instant digital download: one PDF plus a link to a Google Sheet template. Nothing ships.

**Can I print it?**
Yes. It's US Letter and designed to print clean in black and white. You can also annotate it in any PDF app on a phone or tablet.

**Is this legal advice?**
No. It's a practical checklist, photo system, and set of sample emails. Laws and leases vary; check yours and talk to a local tenant resource or attorney for your situation.

**I'm not in Texas. Does it still work?**
Yes. The checklists, photo protocol, cleaning list, and emails work anywhere. The timeline includes a Texas 30-day note and a "check your state" line for everyone else.

**I'm moving tomorrow. Is there time?**
Yes. The cover has a 30–60 minute path: property info, photos, room sheets, keys log, forwarding email.

**My landlord won't do a joint walkthrough or sign anything.**
Common. The kit is built so you can fully document your side alone: dated photos, filled sheets, and emails they received on a known date.

**Is this for landlords too?**
It's tenant-first. Landlords can use the room sheets, but the emails and timeline are written for renters.

**Can I share it with my roommate?**
Yes, within one household. Please don't resell or repost it.

**Refunds?**
Digital product, so sales are generally final. If something's actually wrong with the file, message us and we'll fix it.

**Do you have a version for [state]?**
No, and there isn't one planned. The Texas note is the only state-specific line; the rest applies everywhere.
