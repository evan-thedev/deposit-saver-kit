# Deposit Saver Kit — PDF + Sheet Spec

Builder-facing spec. Draft copy below is a starting point, not final; keep the structure, tighten the words.

Global rules:

- **US Letter (8.5 × 11 in), portrait.** 0.5 in margins minimum. Room sheets may use 0.4 in to fit rows.
- **Prints in black and white.** No color-dependent meaning. Test on a home printer before shipping.
- **Notebook aesthetic:** ruled write-in lines, square checkboxes (□), one display font + one body font max. High contrast (near-black on white). No photos, no gradients, no gray-on-gray.
- **Footer on every page:** `Deposit Saver Kit · Personal use, one household · Not legal advice · Page N of 12`
- **Sheet link** appears on P2 and P12 as a short URL (Evan's domain redirect or a short link) so it survives being read off paper.
- All write-in fields sized for a pen. Minimum 0.28 in row height.

### P2 — How to use

Two columns.

**MOVE-IN (within 48 hours of getting keys)**
1. Fill P3 property info.
2. Read P11 photo protocol. Set your phone to keep location/date metadata on.
3. Walk every room with P4–P8. Fill the **Move-in** column only. Write a Photo ID# for anything that isn't perfect.
4. Photograph meters and every key/fob on P8.
5. Send Email 1 (move-in condition report) from P12 to your landlord the same day. Attach or link the photos.
6. Put the Sheet link in your notes app. Log the photos tonight, not next month.

**MOVE-OUT**
- **7 days before:** Read P9 cleaning list. Confirm your notice was given in writing per your lease. Confirm the key count on P8.
- **Day of:** Clean per P9. Walk every room with P4–P8, fill the **Move-out** column, take the matching photos (same angles as move-in). Photograph meters. Ask for a joint walkthrough; if refused, document your side anyway.
- **After keys are returned:** Send Email 2 (move-out notice + written forwarding address) the same day. Fill the Sheet `Deadline` tab. Calendar the target return date. If nothing arrives, send Email 3.

Sidebar: "Landlord won't sign anything? Fine. Dated photos + your own filled sheets + the emails you sent are still your side of the record."

### P10 — Wear vs. damage cheat sheet

Header: **Normal wear vs. chargeable damage — general guidance, not legal advice.** Subline: "Most states, including Texas, don't allow deductions for normal wear and tear. What counts is fact-specific. Use this to decide what to fix, photograph, and push back on — then check your lease and local rules."

Two-column table, ~14 rows:

| Usually normal wear (not chargeable) | Often chargeable damage |
|---|---|
| Minor scuffs, small nail holes from hanging pictures | Large holes, unapproved paint colors, crayon/marker on walls |
| Faded paint, minor sun-fading of blinds/carpet | Burns, deep gouges, pet damage, torn blinds |
| Carpet worn in traffic paths | Carpet stains, burns, pet urine odor, rips |
| Loose grout or caulk from age | Mold from never running the exhaust fan, broken tiles |
| Worn door hardware, sticky drawer from age | Broken doors, missing hardware, holes from doorknobs |
| Small nicks in countertops | Cuts, burns, chips from dropped items |
| Dusty vents, aged appliance finish | Grease-caked oven, broken appliance parts, missing racks/drip pans |
| Minor scratches on hardwood from furniture | Deep scratches, water damage from plants/pets, unapproved refinishing |
| Lightbulbs burnt out at normal end of life | All bulbs missing/burnt out (petty, but charged) |
| Normal cleaning needed after occupancy | Excessive filth requiring professional cleaning |
| Keys returned | Keys missing → rekey charges |
| Normal wear on window screens | Torn/missing screens |
| Fading of hardware finishes | Rust/damage from leaving items wet on surfaces |
| Small dings in doors/trim | Chipped or cracked doors, trim pulled off |

Footer box: "If a deduction looks like wear, your best tools are your dated move-in photos showing it was already there or your move-out photos showing normal condition. That's why P11 exists."

### P11 — Photo protocol + blank photo log

**Photo protocol (the 8 rules)**
1. Turn on date/time and location in your camera settings. Don't edit or crop the originals.
2. Shoot **wide first, then close**: one wide shot of every wall of every room, then close-ups of anything worth noting.
3. Include a reference in close-ups: a coin, your hand, or a tape measure for scale.
4. Photograph: every wall, floor, ceiling, inside every appliance, inside every cabinet/closet, every window, every fixture, meters, the full key set, the thermostat reading, and the front door with the unit number visible.
5. Take a short video walkthrough too (one continuous take, narrate the date and address at the start). Don't rely on it alone; stills are easier to reference.
6. Name files or log them the same day: `YYYY-MM-DD_Room_Item_##.jpg` (e.g., `2026-09-14_Kitchen_Oven_03.jpg`).
7. Back them up to two places (cloud + email to yourself). The email timestamp is a second date record.
8. Send the landlord a link or the key photos in Email 1 / Email 2 so the date they received them is on record. Log it in the Sheet under "Shared with landlord?"

**Blank photo log** (table, ~20 rows):

| Photo ID# | Date | Room | Item / what it shows | Filename | Shared? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Note: "Photo ID# is what you write in the room sheets. Keep it short: K-03, BR1-07, etc."

### P12 — Timeline + email templates + disclaimer

**Timeline (Texas-aware; check your lease and state)**

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| Move-in, within 48h | Room sheets + photos + Email 1 |
| 30+ days before move-out (or per lease) | Give written notice per lease. Many Texas leases require 30 days' written notice; some require 60. Read yours |
| 7 days before | P9 cleaning, confirm key count, book carpet clean if lease requires |
| Move-out day | Clean, final photos, room sheets (Move-out column), meters, keys returned + photographed |
| Same day | Email 2: move-out notice + written forwarding address |
| Day 30 after keys + forwarding address | Texas: landlord generally has 30 days to refund or send an itemized list of deductions. Calendar this. Other states vary (commonly 14–45 days) |
| Day 31+ | No refund or itemization? Send Email 3. Then look up your local tenant resources (in Texas: Texas Tenant Advisor, local legal aid). Keep everything |

Note under table: "Texas note: the 30-day clock generally does not start until you have both moved out **and** given a written forwarding address. Don't skip Email 2. Withholding your last month's rent because of the deposit is generally not allowed and can cost you more."

**Email 1 — Move-in condition report** (send within 48h of keys)

> Subject: Move-in condition report — [Address, Unit #]
>
> Hi [Name],
> I moved into [Address, Unit #] on [date] and completed a move-in inspection. I've attached/linked dated photos and my inspection notes. Items already present at move-in include: [bullet list, e.g., "scratch on living room floor near window (photo K-02)"].
> Please let me know if you'd like to schedule a joint walkthrough or if you have your own move-in form for me to sign. Thanks,
> [Your name] · [phone]

**Email 2 — Move-out notice and written forwarding address** (send the day keys are returned; also send the notice portion earlier per lease)

> Subject: Move-out confirmation and forwarding address — [Address, Unit #]
>
> Hi [Name],
> I vacated [Address, Unit #] on [date] and returned [number] keys/fobs to [person/location] on [date]. My security deposit of $[amount] was paid on [date].
> Please send my deposit refund and any itemized statement to my forwarding address:
> [Full name]
> [Street, City, State ZIP]
> I've attached/linked dated move-out photos and my inspection notes for your records. Thank you,
> [Your name] · [phone]

**Email 3 — Follow-up after the deadline** (polite, factual; this is not a demand letter)

> Subject: Follow-up: security deposit for [Address, Unit #]
>
> Hi [Name],
> I moved out of [Address, Unit #] on [date] and provided my forwarding address in writing on [date]. As of today ([date], [N] days later) I haven't received the deposit refund or an itemized list of deductions. Could you let me know the status and when I can expect it?
> For reference, my forwarding address is: [address].
> Thanks,
> [Your name] · [phone]

## Google Sheet spec

One Google Sheet, shared **view-only**, distributed via a link that forces "Make a copy" (`/copy` URL). Buyer owns their copy; nothing is shared back with us.

### Tab A — `Photo_Log`

Columns: **Date | Time | Room | Filename | What it shows | Shared with landlord? | Link**

- `Room`: dropdown — Living/Entry, Kitchen, Bedroom 1, Bedroom 2, Bathroom 1, Bathroom 2, Hall/Closets, Laundry, Balcony/Patio, Garage/Storage, Exterior/Door, Meters, Keys
- `Shared with landlord?`: checkbox
- `Link`: plain URL to the cloud copy
- Header row frozen. 100 pre-formatted rows. Alternating row shading (prints fine in grayscale).
- Optional helper column (hidden by default): suggested filename `=TEXT(A2,"yyyy-mm-dd")&"_"&C2&"_"&…` — nice-to-have, not required.

### Tab B — `Deadline`

Vertical key/value layout (one row per field), so it reads on a phone:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Move-out date (keys returned) | date |
| Forwarding address sent in writing? | checkbox |
| Date forwarding address sent | date |
| Deposit amount | currency |
| State deadline days | number, **default 30** (note: "Texas: generally 30. Other states vary; check yours.") |
| **Target return date** | formula: `=IF(AND(B2<>"",B4<>""), MAX(B2,B4)+B6, "")` — later of move-out date and forwarding-sent date, plus deadline days |
| Days remaining | `=IF(B7<>"", B7-TODAY(), "")` |
| Received? | checkbox |
| Amount received | currency |
| Deductions claimed (paste itemization) | long text |
| Notes | long text |

Conditional formatting: Days remaining ≤ 0 and Received unchecked → row highlighted. Simple, visible, no scripts.

### Tab C — `Instructions`

Short text:

1. File → Make a copy (you should already be in your own copy if you used our link).
2. Log photos the same day you take them. Photo ID# in the PDF = filename or row number here.
3. Fill `Deadline` the day you return keys. Calendar the Target return date.
4. This sheet is yours. We can't see it. Nothing syncs anywhere.
5. Not legal advice; personal use, one household. Link back to the disclaimer.

No Apps Script, no external connections, no bank/email integrations. If it needs a script, it's out of scope.
